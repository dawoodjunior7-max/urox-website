THE FORMULATION

Our products originate from a Ukrainian blending facility operating to European lubricant manufacturing standards — Group II and Group III base oils, the same tier used by Shell and Total's premium ranges. The additive packages are developed for South Asian and Middle Eastern operating conditions.

THE TESTING

Every production batch undergoes ASTM-method physical property testing before dispatch. Kinematic viscosity, flash point, pour point, total base number, density — all measured, all documented. We issue a Certificate of Analysis with every shipment. We made it standard practice.

THE DISTRIBUTION

Products are imported into Pakistan and packaged by Bright Lubricants in Karachi, in formats from 1L pails to 208L drums. From there they move to workshops, fleet operators, and distributors across Pakistan and export markets. The supply chain is documented at every step.

THE MISSION

We are building a lubricant brand that international buyers can trust — not because we say so, but because the documentation supports it. Our goal: to become the go-to Ukrainian-formulated lubricant for Middle East, South Asia, and East Africa distribution networks.
